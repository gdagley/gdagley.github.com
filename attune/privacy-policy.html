Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Version 2.1

Our Commitment to Privacy

Attune is designed with your privacy as a core principle. We believe your emotional journey is deeply personal, and we've built our app to ensure it stays that way.

The short version: Your emotion data is stored locally on your device. You may optionally back it up to your own iCloud or Google Drive storage. We collect anonymous usage analytics (no personal data) to improve the app, which you can disable in Settings. Free-tier users see ads powered by Google AdMob, which collects device-level data for ad targeting. Your emotion entries, notes, and tags are never shared with ad networks or any third party.

Data Collection

Attune does not require an account or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or precise location. Google AdMob, which shows ads to free-tier users, receives your IP address and can infer an approximate location from it. Below is a detailed breakdown of what data is collected and why.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

Attune uses Plausible Analytics (self-hosted on the developer's infrastructure) to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

What IS collected:

  • Page/screen views and event counts (e.g. "user tapped insights")
  • Device type and operating system
  • Country (derived from IP, which is not stored)

What is NOT collected:

  • Your emotion entries, notes, or tags
  • IP addresses (discarded after country lookup)
  • Cookies or persistent identifiers
  • Any personal data

You can disable analytics at any time in the app's Settings > Data & Privacy section.

Advertising

Attune displays banner advertisements on certain screens (Insights, History, and Learn pages) for free-tier users via Google AdMob. Pro owners do not see ads.

Data collected by AdMob:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location is inferred
  • Usage data and app interaction metrics
  • Diagnostics (crash data, performance metrics)

This data is collected by Google to serve ads. Before any ads load, free-tier users are asked two things. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Attune shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. On iOS, Attune then asks for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. Pro owners are never asked, because they see no ads. If you were shown the consent form, you can change your choices at any time while you see ads, in Settings > Data & Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. You can change the tracking choice at any time in your device's Settings.

Important: Your emotion entries, notes, tags, and other personal content are never shared with ad networks or any third party. The data boundary between your emotion data and advertising is strictly enforced.

In-App Purchases

Attune offers two optional purchases through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store: a one-time Pro upgrade, and a Tip Jar, a one-time tip that unlocks nothing. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by the respective platform store.

  • Purchase verification is performed through the platform's standard APIs
  • No sensitive payment details, tokens, or financial information are stored on your device
  • Only your Pro status (a simple yes/no flag) is cached locally to enable features

Data Storage

Attune follows a local-first approach. All data you enter—including your emotion entries, notes, tags, and preferences—is stored on your device using a local database.

Cloud Backup (Optional)

You may optionally enable cloud backup to protect your data:

  • iOS: Backs up to your personal iCloud storage (iCloud Documents container). No additional sign-in is required.
  • Android: Backs up to your personal Google Drive storage (app-specific folder). Requires Google Sign-In for authentication.

All backup data is transmitted over TLS (encrypted in transit). Attune does not operate any backend servers. Your data goes directly from your device to your own cloud storage—we never see, access, or process your data in transit or at rest.

If cloud backup is disabled, your data remains exclusively on your device. If you delete the app without a backup, all your data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

Attune integrates the following third-party services:

  • Plausible Analytics (self-hosted) — Privacy-focused, cookie-free usage analytics. No personal data is collected. Data is stored on the developer's own infrastructure, not shared with any third party. You can opt out in Settings.
  • Google AdMob — Advertising SDK that collects device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics for ad targeting. Ads are shown to free-tier users only. Your emotion data is never shared with AdMob.
  • Apple iCloud (iOS) — Used for optional cloud backup of your emotion data to your own iCloud storage.
  • Google Drive (Android) — Used for optional cloud backup of your emotion data to your own Google Drive storage (app-specific folder).
  • Google Sign-In (Android) — Used to authenticate access to Google Drive for cloud backup.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — Used for in-app purchase verification of the Pro upgrade.

Permissions

Attune may request or use the following device permissions:

  • Notifications: Optional. Used only to send you reminder notifications that you configure within the app. No data is sent externally.
  • Advertising ID: Used by AdMob for ad targeting. You can limit or reset this identifier through your device's privacy settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • iCloud entitlement (iOS): Used for cloud backup. This is an app capability and does not require a separate user prompt.
  • Google Drive scope (Android): Used for cloud backup. Access is limited to an app-specific folder only—Attune cannot read or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other sensitive permissions.

Children's Privacy

Attune is rated 12+ on app stores. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob is configured as not child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: Your data is always accessible within the app
  • Export: Pro users can export their data as a CSV file via Settings > Export Data
  • Deletion: Delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall to remove all local data. If cloud backup is enabled, you can delete your backup data from your iCloud or Google Drive storage directly.
  • Analytics opt-out: Disable anonymous analytics at any time in Settings
  • Ad tracking: Allow or stop tracking at any time in your device's Settings (on iOS, Privacy & Security > Tracking)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your data. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what data is collected, to delete your data, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. Attune does not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
