6-Day Fat Loss + Muscle Plan

5–25 lb dumbbells · treadmill · up to 60 min/day

How to use

Tap an exercise to expand setup, technique, form cues, common mistakes, easier options, progression, and a video link. Check exercises off as you go; completion is saved on this device.

Progression rule

Finish most strength sets with about 1–2 good reps still possible. When you can hit the top of the rep range on every set with good form, increase the dumbbell weight. Once 25 lb is easy, slow the lowering phase, add a pause, or use a single-leg / single-arm variation.

Use controlled, pain-free movement. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath.
