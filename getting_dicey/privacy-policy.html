Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

Getting Dicey is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect or store any personal data. Your settings and dice are stored locally on your device.

Anonymous Analytics

Getting Dicey collects anonymous usage analytics to help us understand which features are popular and improve the app. Specifically:

  • We track which actions are used (e.g., rolling dice, changing settings) and basic properties (e.g., die type, dice count)
  • No personal information, device identifiers, or IP addresses are stored
  • No cookies or advertising identifiers are used
  • No account or registration is required

Analytics are powered by Plausible, a privacy-focused analytics platform. You can opt out at any time in Settings > About > Privacy > Anonymous Analytics.

Advertising

Getting Dicey displays banner and interstitial ads to free users via Google AdMob. Specifically:

  • AdMob collects the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS if ATT consent is granted, GAID on Android) for ad serving
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps) is collected by Google
  • Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change these choices at any time while you see ads, in Settings > About > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices
  • On iOS, the app then asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission when it starts, before the advertising identifier is used. If you decline, ads are not personalized. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings
  • Users who have purchased Remove Ads or Pro are never asked, because they see no ads
  • Ads are removed entirely when you purchase the Remove Ads or Pro upgrade

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Getting Dicey offers in-app purchases (Remove Ads, Pro and a Tip Jar) to enhance your experience. All transactions are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Specifically:

  • The app does not access or store any payment information
  • Purchase state (whether you have purchased an upgrade) is stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences
  • Purchase verification is done against store receipts — no data is sent to our servers

Cloud Sync

For Pro users, cloud sync keeps dice bag data the same across devices. On iOS it syncs through your own iCloud account. On Android it syncs through your own Google Drive once you sign in with Google.

  • iOS: Dice bag data syncs via iCloud
  • Android: Dice bag data syncs via Google Drive, which requires Google Sign-In authentication
  • Data synced includes dice bag configurations (names, dice setups, and timestamps)
  • No data is sent to Getting Dicey servers — sync goes directly to your own iCloud or Google Drive account

Data Storage

Your settings, preferences, and purchase state are stored locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences). This data is not accessible to us or any third party. Pro users who enable cloud sync will also have their dice bag data stored in their own iCloud or Google Drive account — see the Cloud Sync section above for details.

Third-Party Services

Getting Dicey uses the following third-party services:

  • Plausible Analytics — self-hosted instance for anonymous usage analytics. No personal data is collected. Analytics events are sent only when a network connection is available.
  • Google AdMob — ad serving for free users. Collects device advertising identifier and ad interaction data. See the Advertising section above for details.
  • Google Sign-In — used for Google Drive authentication on Android. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • iCloud — used for cloud sync on iOS for Pro users.
  • Google Drive — used for cloud sync on Android for Pro users.

Children's Privacy

Getting Dicey does not target children and is not designed for use by children under 13. While AdMob is present for ad serving, the app does not use COPPA-restricted ad settings. Anonymous analytics can be disabled in settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@gettingdicey.com.
